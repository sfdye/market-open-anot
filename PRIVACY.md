# Privacy Policy

**Open Anot?**
Last updated: 16 August 2026

## Summary

This app collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no server operated by the developer. Everything the app knows about you stays on your device.

## What is stored on your device

- The markets you have chosen to follow
- Your language preference and whether reminders are turned on
- A cached copy of the public closure dataset, so the app works offline

This data is held in the app's own storage and is removed when you delete the app. It is never uploaded anywhere.

## Location

Location access is optional. If you grant it, your coordinates are used on the device to sort markets by how close they are and to centre the map. Your location is never transmitted, stored, or shared. The app works fully without it — you can search and browse markets by name instead.

## Notifications

Closure reminders are scheduled locally by iOS or Android on your device. No push server is involved and no device token is sent anywhere.

## Network requests

The app makes requests to these third parties, none of which receive any personal information from the app beyond the network request itself (which necessarily includes your IP address, as with any web request):

- **data.gov.sg** — to download the "Dates of Hawker Centre Closure" dataset published by Singapore's National Environment Agency
- **NEA image servers** — to load the photo shown on a market's detail screen
- **onemap.gov.sg** — to load map tiles when you open the map

These services have their own privacy practices, which the developer does not control.

## Children

No personal data is collected from anyone, including children.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be published in this file, and its history is visible in the repository's commit log.
