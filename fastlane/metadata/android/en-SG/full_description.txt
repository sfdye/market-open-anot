Check if your Singapore hawker centre or wet market is open or closed — today and every day.

Open Anot? covers all 123 NEA-managed hawker centres and wet markets. It detects Monday rest days, quarterly cleaning closures, and other maintenance shutdowns, so you always know before you go.

• Instant open/closed status for the markets you follow
• Upcoming closures list with the next reopening date
• Local closure reminders: 7pm the evening before and 6am the morning of
• Map of every market, sorted by distance when you share your location
• Tap an address to open it in Google Maps
• Bilingual: English and Chinese
• Works offline — the dataset is cached and revalidated in the background
• Senior-friendly: large text support, high contrast, light and dark

No account, no backend, no tracking. The app fetches the NEA closure schedule directly and does everything on your device.
