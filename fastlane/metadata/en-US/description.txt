Check if your Singapore hawker centre or wet market is open or closed — today and every day. No more wasted trips.

Open Anot? covers every publicly listed hawker centre and wet market in Singapore. It shows you upcoming closure dates — quarterly cleaning, maintenance shutdowns, and other rest days — and reminds you before they happen, so you always know before you go.

• Instant open/closed status for the markets you follow
• Upcoming closures list with the next reopening date
• Local closure reminders: 7pm the evening before and 6am the morning of
• Map of every market, sorted by distance when you share your location
• Tap an address to open it in Apple Maps or Google Maps
• Bilingual: English and Chinese
• Works offline — the dataset is cached and revalidated in the background
• Senior-friendly: honours Dynamic Type at every size, high contrast, light and dark

This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government agency.
